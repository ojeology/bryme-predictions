## Note on the card board and team cards

A team's card record is mostly the referee's doing, and it mean-reverts hard. Walk-forward, teams with a prior rate under 0.9 cards a game went on to average **1.49**; teams with a prior rate over 2.6 went on to average **2.39**, not the 2.90 they had been getting. A model that bet the team effect lost to simply betting the league rate on every card line. **So: hunt cards by referee, never by team reputation.**
