## HOW THE MODEL WAS BUILT (plain English)

- Every match is predicted from matches that kicked off EARLIER only. No same-day result is ever used, no future result is ever used.
- Form = points per game over the last 5 matches for both sides, plus a HOT/COLD flag (away hot, home cold) because that is the case that pays.
- The rating part is the goals model (Poisson + Dixon-Coles + ridge), refitted on every matchday with everything known before it.
- The penalty on the form model was chosen on a hold-out (first half of the season scored on the second half), never on the data it was then scored against.
- Walk-forward record over 1268 matches: rating model alone log loss 1.0352, with form 1.0364; accuracy 47.0% -> 46.7%.
